// NARRATIVE BIOGRAPHY

From Ethiopia to Computational Biology

Hi, I'm Fenan.

I'm an aspiring computational scientist, builder, and first-generation college student studying Computational Biology and Entrepreneurship at Brown University. At the heart of what I do is simple: finding connections across disciplines and understanding how complex systems work.

I was born and fed in Ethiopia, where I grew up herding cattle in a small town in western Ethiopia (believed to be the region where coffee originated). Growing up so closely connected to animals and nature, I became fascinated by the way the world around me (and the human body within it) has adapted, persisted, and evolved through the test of time. That early sense of fascination continues to shape the questions I ask today.

Now, as a sophomore in Computational Biology on the Biological Sciences Track, I am drawn to the quantitative side of biology: scientific computing, biomedical research, vaccine development, pharmacology, and the engineering of biological systems. My interest in this work became deeply personal during the COVID-19 pandemic, when I lost a relative to the virus. Experiencing the consequences of an unpredictable pandemic firsthand bolstered my desire to contribute to research that helps us understand disease, anticipate biological threats, and develop better interventions.

I am especially interested in the computational journey from genomic sequence data and biological measurements to models, discoveries, and eventually real-world solutions across any field. More broadly, I want to build the quantitative and interdisciplinary toolkit needed to tackle complex problems wherever biology, computation, engineering, and human health intersect.

My long-term goal is to grow into an interdisciplinary physician-scientist, translating discoveries made through computational and experimental biology into meaningful advances in medicine.

Outside the laboratory, I'm an avid nature lover who enjoys hiking, morning walks, watching science-fiction films, and (perhaps most of all) meeting new people. Some of my favorite ideas have come from conversations with people who think completely differently from me. I thrive in collaborative environments and am always excited to connect with curious minds.
