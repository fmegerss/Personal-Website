PROJECT 02

Heroneck Pipeline

Overview: An automated data processing pipeline built to quantify rapid anatomical movements and joint covariance during Great Egret strike behaviors.

The Problem

High-speed strike mechanics in wading birds require frame-by-frame anatomical measurement. Manually tracking neck curvature and segment velocity across video sets is time-consuming, subjective, and difficult to standardize when analyzing statistical covariance across joint dynamics.

What Was Solved

  • Automated Feature Tracking: Streamlined marker and joint tracking across high-speed video frames to eliminate manual annotation bottlenecks.
  • Covariance Quantification: Converted spatial positioning over time into statistical covariance metrics, isolating key anatomical dependencies during strikes.
  • Data Standardization: Produced reproducible numerical data outputs ready for downstream biomechanical analysis and visualization.

Demonstration of automated keypoint extraction and kinematic tracking.
